# Battleship Shot Patterns

An animated guide to hunting ships in Battleship. Space your shots by the length of the smallest ship your opponent still has afloat:

| Smallest ship left | Pattern | Shots to cover a 10 × 10 board |
|---|---|---|
| 2 (Destroyer) | Checkerboard | 50 |
| 3 (Cruiser or Submarine) | Every 3rd diagonal | 33 |
| 4 (Battleship) | Every 4th diagonal | 24 |
| 5 (Carrier) | Every 5th diagonal | 20 |

Live page: https://nartc.github.io/slop-battleship-move/

The whole site is a single `index.html` with no build step.
